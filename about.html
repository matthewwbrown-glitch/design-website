Hi there!

I'm Matthew Brown, currently working at Meta as a product designer, where I lead design across the Shopify Meta App and Advertiser Experience (AdEx).

I have experience in user testing, developing wireframes, agile and lean UX, branding, advertising and digital design. I've worked with some of the best creative agencies, brands and tech companies, and tutored User Experience Design at General Assembly. I put this page together so the people I work with (and might work with) can understand me a bit better.

How I Work

"I hold quality to an uncompromising bar."

I live in the product, test relentlessly, and catch edge cases long before they reach customers. I view design reviews and hands-on dogfooding not as optional checkpoints, but as core practices that safeguard the end-to-end user experience. Quality isn't a single project phase. It's how I show up every day.

"I'm a systems thinker by default."

I build foundations that make future problems easier to solve, whether that's reusable component libraries, single-source-of-truth documentation, or operational frameworks for cross-functional teams. I believe clear documentation and shared infrastructure are just as much a design output as high-fidelity prototypes.

"I thrive in ambiguity."

When priorities shift or team boundaries blur, I step into the gap. I excel at taking ill-defined problems, conducting strategic audits, and establishing structure where there is none. I am most effective when dropped into complex, high-stakes environments that require fast synthesis and steady execution.

"I make work visible."

Knowledge shared is impact multiplied. Whether through design office hours, team workshops, internal newsletters, or strategic recaps, I proactively communicate progress and insights across organizational boundaries. If I figure out a better way to do something, I make sure the whole team benefits.

"I invest in the people around me."

Design is fundamentally a team effort. I prioritize mentoring emerging designers, championing inclusive design initiatives, and supporting cross-functional partners through fast-moving transitions. I care just as much about building healthy team cultures as I do about shipping great products.
