Profile

Melbourne-born. Seattle-based.

Matthew Brown — Seattle

I work at Meta in Seattle as a product designer, where I lead design across NCE (New Commerce Experiences) and the Advertiser Experiences team.

I design simple products for complex systems.

As a Lead Product Designer with over 15 years of global experience spanning Australia and the U.S., I’ve worked in creative agencies, top-tier tech companies (Atlassian, Meta), and digital platforms. I take on dense, data-heavy workflows head-on and transform them into seamless, high-conversion tools.

At Meta, I’ve spent the last 6+ years spearheading ad creation funnels, merchant success frameworks, and advertiser experiences that have unlocked billions in value for the advertisers and sellers who use them.

I’m relentlessly curious, enjoy working across functions, and bring ideas to life by blending analog sketching with ever-changing AI prototyping tools to build products that scale.

How I Work

I hold quality to an uncompromising bar, think in systems by default and thrive in ambiguity. I live in the product, build foundations that make the next problem easier to solve, and bring structure where there is none, whether that's aligning cross-functional teams in a workshop, tying design decisions to business outcomes or mentoring the designers around me.

Influences & Craft

  • The Analog Baseline: Whether shooting on my Leica M6 and Rollei 35 T or sketching in Leuchtturm1917 journals, physical mediums keep me grounded. Every frame and line teaches discipline.
  • AI & Rapid Tinkering: I actively explore and test the boundaries of AI-assisted design tools, using them as collaborative partners to push conceptual exploration further.
  • Living the Product: I don’t just design for the ideal path; I dogfood the products I build to understand real-world friction and edge cases firsthand.
  • Mentoring & Teaching: From General Assembly to Bridge to Tech, I love helping aspiring designers turn raw curiosity into rigorous craft.
