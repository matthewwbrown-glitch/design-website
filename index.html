Principles

  1. Craft

    Design quality isn't a setting you dial in. It's a standard you hold every day.

  2. Systems

    The best solution also makes the next ten solutions easier.

  3. Clarity

    Complexity is rarely the right answer. My job is to reduce friction, not add it.

  4. Curiosity

    I'm always exploring new tools and ways of working, and sharing what I find with the team.

Profile

Melbourne-born. Seattle-based.

Matthew Brown — Seattle

I work (remotely) at Meta in Seattle as a product designer, where I lead design across NCE (New Commerce Experiences) and the Advertiser Experiences team.

I design simple products for complex systems.

As a Lead Product Designer with over 15 years of experience spanning creative agencies, top-tier tech companies (Atlassian, Meta), and digital platforms, my focus is on taking dense, data-heavy workflows and transforming them into seamless, high-converting tools.

At Meta, I’ve spent the last 6+ years spearheading ad creation funnels, merchant success frameworks, and advertiser experiences that have unlocked billions in platform value. I believe in relentless curiosity, cross-functional collaboration, and the power of rigorous design thinking—blending analog sketching with modern AI prototyping to build products that scale.

How I Work

I hold quality to an uncompromising bar, think in systems by default and thrive in ambiguity. I live in the product, build foundations that make the next problem easier to solve, and bring structure where there is none, whether that's aligning cross-functional teams in a workshop, tying design decisions to business outcomes or mentoring the designers around me.

Influences
