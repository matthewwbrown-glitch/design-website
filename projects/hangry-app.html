General Assembly UX Capstone · 2014

When you're hungry + angry

A late night conversation between friends turned into the project that changed everything.

The Challenge

This project was completed while studying UX design at General Assembly in 2014. Hangry tries to solve the problem of what to do when you're hungry and angry... Hangry?

One of many class affinity diagram sessions. Elevator pitch sketch explaining my project.

Sketching, testing and prototyping

Throughout the project I conducted guerrilla testing with paper prototypes and user surveys.

Guerrilla testing — Conducted user testing with paper prototypes and the 'POP' app on the iPhone.

POP prototype — The POP app revealed some major usability issues that I rectified for further testing.

Design Studio Method

Conducted Design Studio Method testing with six participants. The question asked: What do you do when you're tired/angry and hungry — Hangry?

Round 1 — Each participant presented their solutions to the group. They ranked their favourite ideas with red sticker dots.

Round 2 — Further refinements were made then presented back to the group. The three standout ideas were chosen.
